02 / About Me

Hey, I'm Arman

Hi, I'm Arman Lamba, a passionate Video Editor with over 2 years of experience in creating engaging and impactful videos. I primarily work with Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects to turn raw footage into content that is both visually appealing and strategically edited.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to work with some amazing creators and professionals from around the world, including collaborating with a top YouTube strategist from the USA. I've also worked alongside Top Rated editors on Upwork and contributed to projects with leading agencies from India and Australia.

Through these experiences, I've learned that great editing is not just about cuts and effects — it's about storytelling, pacing, and keeping the audience engaged.

My goal is simple: Create videos that not only look great but also perform. Whether it's YouTube videos, podcasts, social media content, or cinematic edits, I focus on delivering high-quality edits that help creators and brands grow.
